Overview

Trade Post adds a full economic system to MineColonies built around Trade Coins. Sell items at your Marketplace, trade between colonies via rail, send citizens on vacation, train pets for automated tasks, recycle materials, and establish remote outposts.

Removing this mod after use in a colony can corrupt the colony. If you need to remove it, deconstruct all Trade Post buildings first but keep the JAR installed.

Buildings

Marketplace
Shopkeeper
The economic foundation. Sell items as souvenirs to colony visitors for Trade Coins. At level 2+, mint Bronze Trade Coins. Configure sellable items and watch the coins roll in.

Revenue formula: item value × (1 + primary_skill / 50)
Visitor shopping chance: building_level × (shoppingChance/100 + research_modifier)
No research required — Build first!
Train Station
Station Master
Inter-colony trade hub. Connect colonies by rail for import/export. Increases colony visitor count. Ghost minecarts travel the tracks during shipments.

Imports per level: 5 (configurable)
Trade speed: 4 per building level
Visitor boost: random(10 × level) + 15
Expansionism — Marketplace L1 + 16 rails + 1 Trade Coin
Resort
Guest Services Bartender
Citizens with burned-out stats go on vacation here. Guest Services diagnoses the issue and delivers remedy items. Bartender mixes drinks that help vacationers relax.

Guest capacity: 2 per level (level 5 = 10 guests)
Healing: +2 stat points per vacation
Income: 1 Trade Coin per vacation
Staycations — Marketplace L2 + 1 Trade Coin
Recycling Center
Recycling Engineer
Breaks down unwanted items into raw components. Processes souvenirs, uses reverse-engineered crafting recipes, and can strip enchantments (with research).

Capacity: 2 processes per level (level 5 = 10)
Cycle time: 100 ticks (configurable)
Perfect recycling at: skill level 50
Environmentalism — Marketplace L1 + 5 Trade Coins
Pet Shop
Animal Trainer
Train wolves, foxes, axolotls, and parrots for colony tasks: herding, scavenging, dredging, hauling. With Husbandry research, also procure farm animals.

Pet capacity: 2 per level (level 5 = 10 pets)
Training cost: 16 items + 3 Trade Coins per pet
Best Friends — Marketplace L1 + 16 bowls
Outpost
Scout
Remote colony extension up to 800 blocks away. Access different biomes for crops. Includes sub-buildings: Builder, Farm, Guard towers. Connected by rail.

Max distance: 800 blocks (configurable)
Sub-buildings: Builder, Farm, Guard Left, Guard Right
Outpost — Station L3 + 1 Outpost Claim + 3 Gold Coins

Recommended Progression

Build a Marketplace — no research needed. This is your first building and the foundation of the entire economy.
Start selling items — configure sellable items in the Marketplace hut. Shopkeeper converts them to souvenirs and sells to visitors.
Research Capitalism — requires Tavern L1 + Marketplace L1. Costs 5 gold ingots. Unlocks advertising upgrades.
Mint Trade Coins — at Marketplace L2+, sneak-right-click the hut with an Advanced Clipboard, or use the economic tab.
Research Expansionism — costs 16 rails + 1 Trade Coin. Build a Train Station to boost visitors and enable inter-colony trade.
Research Staycations — requires Marketplace L2. Build a Resort when citizens show burnout symptoms.
Research Environmentalism — costs 5 Trade Coins. Build a Recycling Center for breaking down excess materials.
Research Best Friends — costs 16 bowls. Build a Pet Shop to train pets for herding, scavenging, and hauling.
Build a Wishing Well — 2×2 water pool surrounded by Mixed Stone. Throw coins + items for rituals.
Research Outpost — requires Station L3 + Tourism research. Establish remote outposts for biome-diverse farming (late game).

Citizen Burnout & Vacation System

Citizens with overworked (low) stats become susceptible to Resort advertising. When saturation exceeds the threshold, they take a vacation. Guest Services diagnoses the specific burned-out stat and delivers the correct remedy items.
